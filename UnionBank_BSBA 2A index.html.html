About Union Bank

UnionBank is a publicly-listed universal bank that distinguishes itself through superior technology, unique branch sales and service culture, and centralized backroom operations. It provides a wide range of banking products and services to both retail and corporate clients.

As one of the leading universal banks in the Philippines, UnionBank is known for its digital innovations and technological advancements in banking. The Bank is a joint venture among the Aboitiz Group, Insular Life, and Social Security System.

Head Office

UnionBank Plaza, Meralco Avenue corner Onyx Road, Ortigas Center, Pasig City, Philippines 1605

Our History

1968

Union Bank of the Philippines (UnionBank) was established on August 16, 1968, as a commercial bank.

1992

Received its universal banking license from the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas, enabling it to provide expanded banking services.

1994

Acquisition of International Corporate Bank, strengthening its market presence.

2006

Acquisition of International Exchange Bank, further expanding its operations.

2022

Acquisition of Citibank's consumer banking business in the Philippines, significantly enhancing its retail banking capabilities.

Capital Composition

UnionBank's capital structure consists of:

  • Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1): Common shares, retained earnings, and other comprehensive income
  • Additional Tier 1 (AT1): Preferred shares and other qualifying instruments
  • Tier 2 Capital: General loan loss provisions and subordinated debt
Dividend Policy

UnionBank implements a progressive dividend policy that balances the need for capital retention to support growth and providing returns to shareholders. The bank targets a dividend payout ratio of 30% of the previous year's net income, subject to regulatory requirements and capital planning considerations.
